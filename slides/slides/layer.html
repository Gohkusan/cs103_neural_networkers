[Zyik, about 45 s] Now take many neurons and stack their weight vectors as the rows of one matrix W. Then all of their dot products happen at once: z equals W a plus b. That is exactly the Week 2 row picture: entry i of the result is row i dotted with the input. The column picture says the same product is a linear combination of W's columns, weighted by the input entries. Check the shapes under each symbol: n_l by n_(l-1) times n_(l-1) by 1 gives n_l by 1, which conforms. The superscript in parentheses is the layer number, never a power. Then phi is applied to every entry separately.
