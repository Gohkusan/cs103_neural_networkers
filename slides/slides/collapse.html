[Voon, about 50 s] Why do we need phi at all? Remove it and expand two layers: W2 times (W1 x plus b1) plus b2 regroups into one matrix W2 W1 times x, plus one vector. So a hundred layers without phi are worth exactly one layer. Strictly, W x plus b is affine, not linear, because it sends zero to b; Week 1's definition of linear needs f(0) = 0. On our numbers: with the identity activation the network equals the single map [-1 -1] x + 2, giving 2, 1, 1, 0, not XOR. Week 5 adds that rank can only shrink through a product, so a narrow layer is a bottleneck. And Week 10 least squares shows the best affine fit to XOR predicts 0.5 everywhere. If asked: the homogeneous trick appends a 1 to x so the affine map becomes one bigger linear map, and stacking layers is block matrix multiplication.
