Three linear-algebra operations power deep learning

Matrix product

Z = WA + b1ᵀ

Forward pass and batching; Wᵀ carries errors back.

Outer product

∂ℓ/∂W = δaᵀ

Each sample’s weight gradient has rank 1.

Hadamard product

[Wᵀδ] ⊙ φ′(z)

The bend’s slope, entry by entry.

And the bend φ is what makes depth worth having.

[Zyik, about 20 s] To sum up: three Week 2 operations do all the work. Matrix products run the forward pass, handle whole batches at once, and with a transpose carry errors backwards. Outer products give each weight gradient, rank 1 per sample. Hadamard products apply the slope of the bend entry by entry. And the bend itself is what stops all those layers collapsing into one.
