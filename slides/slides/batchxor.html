[Voon, about 45 s] Here is our running example: 2 inputs, 2 hidden ReLU neurons, 1 output, on XOR. Instead of four separate matrix-vector products, we put the four inputs side by side as the columns of X, a 2 by 4 matrix. One product W X gives all four pre-activations at once. The bias has to be added to every column; that copy is the outer product b times 1-transpose, from Week 2. ReLU just zeroes the negative entries. The output layer is the row vector [1, -2], and the outputs are 0, 1, 1, 0, which is exactly XOR. These weights are the textbook solution from Goodfellow, Bengio and Courville, section 6.1; we recomputed every number.
